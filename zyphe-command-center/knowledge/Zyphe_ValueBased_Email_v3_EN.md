## What changed (so you can see the feedback applied)
Hendrik's note: every benefit needs the layer underneath it, what it actually means to the person, written so they don't have to think about it. The steak has protein; what does protein do for *you*. So every claim now carries its "so you..." payoff aimed at the reader's day, not the product.

One example of the change:
- **Before:** "AI agents run KYC, KYB and AML on their own, so your team approves instead of doing every check by hand."
- **After:** "AI agents run KYC, KYB and AML on their own, so instead of being the bottleneck everyone waits on, your team just approves the cases and clears the backlog that's been hanging over the next audit."

Same fact, but the reader feels it without having to translate it.

## 5) How to make it more *you* (Edoardo's voice)
The drafts are deliberately clean so Hendrik can review the structure. Before you send, rough them up so they sound like you, not like a template. Concrete ways:

- **Anchor on where you actually met them.** Half these contacts you met in person or spoke to directly. "We met at Money 2020 in Vegas" or "we spoke last autumn about [X]" beats "since we last spoke" every time, and it's true. Put the real memory in.
- **Say the one thing you remember about their problem.** If you recall their specific pain from the first conversation, lead with it. That's the ultimate "deeper layer," you already know what it means to them.
- **Let a little of your accent through.** You're Italian writing to a mixed market. A slightly less polished, more direct line reads as human, not as an AI draft. Don't over-sand it.
- **One short sentence of you.** A single honest line ("I kept your details because I genuinely think this fits what you were building") lands warmer than any benefit. You're persistent and relationship-driven, that's a strength, use it.
- **Match the channel to the relationship.** For the warmest ones, a LinkedIn voice note or a two-line personal message will beat a formatted email. Use the sequence for volume, break format for the ones you actually know.
- **Keep your sign-off consistent** across email, LinkedIn and calls so the follow-ups feel like one person staying in touch, which is exactly what you've done with Banco Inter for a year.
