## What changed vs v2 (the gaps the call scripts were missing)
- **The liability wedge.** We operate the workflow as Compliance-as-a-Service and take on **defined regulatory liability for the operated scope**. No other IDV vendor does this. This is the strongest line in the whole pitch and it was not in the call scripts.
- **Agents that finish, not flag.** Sharper than "we run KYC": agents complete L1/L2 reviews (sanctions/PEP, KYC/KYB, AML investigations) **end to end**, with a **per-decision rationale and audit-ready log**, and escalate **only the ambiguous cases** to a human.
- **KYC Passport.** A reusable, user-held credential: verify once, reuse across flows, one-click re-verification instead of another upload.
- **No PII honeypot, said properly.** Decentralized, sharded, encrypted vault, PII stays off your servers. Plus SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR.
- **Real competitor facts** for the competitor hook, instead of a blank [X]%.
- **Objection handling** on the call.

## The core pitch, in one breath
"Zyphe's AI agents work inside your existing compliance stack and finish L1/L2 reviews end to end, sanctions/PEP, KYC/KYB, AML investigations, each with an audit-ready rationale. Your team only sees the ambiguous cases. And unlike every other vendor, we take on defined regulatory liability for the scope we operate."

## The one-two punch to land on every call (no competitor can say either)
1. Agents complete the work end to end with audit-ready logs; humans only get escalations.
2. Zyphe carries **defined regulatory liability** for the operated scope. Software vendors hand you a tool and the risk; we take both.
Get these two in before you ask for the meeting.

## OBJECTION HANDLING (short answer, then back to the ask)
========================================
- **"We already have a KYC/AML tool."** Keep it. Our agents work inside your existing stack. The difference is the work gets finished, not just flagged. Worth 20 minutes to see it on your own queue?
- **"AI can't make compliance decisions."** Agreed, not alone. Every decision has a written rationale and audit log, ambiguous cases go to a human, and we take liability for the scope we run. That last part is the real answer. Can I show you how the escalation logic works?
- **"How can a vendor take regulatory liability?"** It's contractually defined per scope, and it's exactly why this is worth a call rather than an email. 20 minutes this week?
- **"Just send me a deck."** Happy to, and I will. But the escalation logic and the liability model only make sense live on a real case. Can I grab 15 minutes and send the deck as follow-up?
- **"We're worried about a vendor breach."** That's the point of the architecture. PII is sharded and encrypted and stays off your servers, so there's no central honeypot to breach. SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001. Worth 15 minutes to walk the security model?

## COMPETITOR HOOK — real facts (use when they mention or use a competitor)
========================================
Rule: **position categories, not names** ("tools that flag" vs "agents that finish"). Don't bash by name unless they name it first. Never claim unlimited liability, always "defined regulatory liability for the operated scope." Use a competitor signal as a reason to move faster to a meeting.

- **Sumsub:** disclosed incident Jan 2026; 20-30% higher per-verification than us for a comparable mix; sells software you operate. Hook: "worth 15 minutes to compare on breach risk and cost, side by side?"
- **Veriff:** confirmed breach Nov 2025, government IDs exposed; one fixed KYC shape; SaaS. Hook: no central store makes that architecturally impossible with us.
- **Persona:** dropped by Discord Feb 2026 over a security incident; enterprise-gated six-figure minimums; orchestration you still operate.
- **Jumio:** named in multiple US BIPA biometric lawsuits; heavy workforce cuts in 2024-25; legacy one-size-fits-all.
- **Onfido / Entrust:** acquisition limbo since 2024, roadmap reset, central PII store.
- **IDnow:** EU-narrow, slow video-call IDV, central PII store.
- **Yoti / Shufti:** privacy-positioned or broad SaaS, but they stop at the check; we operate the AML casework and share liability. Don't attack Yoti on privacy, win on operated workflow and liability.
- **Didit (partner, not replace):** strong IDV primitive, SaaS only, no operated workflow or liability. Play: "Didit verifies; Zyphe operates the AML review, monitoring and audit-evidence work on top, with liability we take on."
- **miniOrange:** access-management vendor (SSO/MFA) with an IDV add-on, not a purpose-built compliance operator. Only win when the need is regulated KYC/KYB/AML.

## HARD RULES FOR THE CALL (from the messaging doc)
========================================
- One idea, one ask. Lead with their situation or a trigger, never with Zyphe.
- No em dashes, no filler ("just circling back," "leverage," "seamless," "game-changer," etc.).
- Do not fear-monger or imply they're non-compliant.
- Never pitch an MLRO or Head of Compliance on replacing their team, only on capacity and defensibility.
- Never name-drop clients or metrics you weren't given. Web3 references (Supra, Protocol Labs, Yescoin) are proof of scale/architecture, not peers for regulated buyers.
- Always end on one clear ask for a 15-20 minute call, and send the booking link as follow-up.
